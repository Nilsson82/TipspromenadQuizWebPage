# Starter quiz content sources

Facts checked against these educational or scientific institutions on 2026-09-10. The questions, answers and explanations are original short formulations rather than copied source text.

- `triangle-sides`: A triangle has three sides. [OpenStax, Elementary Algebra 2e, section 3.4](https://openstax.org/books/elementary-algebra-2e/pages/3-4-solve-geometry-applications-triangles-rectangles-and-the-pythagorean-theorem).
- `water-formula`: Water consists of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom. [USGS, Interactive Water Cycle Diagram for Kids](https://water.usgs.gov/edu/watercycle-kids-adv.html).
- `planet-nearest-sun`: Mercury is the nearest planet to the Sun. [NASA, Mercury Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mercury/facts/).
- `earth-natural-satellite`: The Moon orbits Earth as its natural satellite. [NASA, Moon Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/).
- `photosynthesis-gas`: Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and light. [NOAA, How has the ocean made life on land possible?](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/oceanproduction/). Water is shown as an input in [NOAA, Carbon Dioxide Lesson](https://sos.noaa.gov/media/downloads/sosx/climate_change_and_carbon_dioxide_lesson.pdf).
- `spider-legs`: Spiders have eight legs; insects have six. [Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, What Is an Insect?](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/education/teaching-resources/life-science/what-insect).

Language coverage: English (`en`), Swedish (`sv`), Spanish (`es`), Danish (`da`), Norwegian Bokmål (`no`) and Finnish (`fi`). All six languages contain all six starter questions, with the same logical question and answer IDs. Mathematical numerals and chemical formulas intentionally remain the same across languages. Translations have not been reviewed by native-speaking editors.

The historical question banks are separate content, not complete translations of this starter quiz or of one another. Their availability must be shown honestly. They contain dated or ambiguous questions that need an editorial review before they can be described as fact-checked.
